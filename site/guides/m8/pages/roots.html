Patch card · Macrosynth · low roots

Roots

A soft, nearly pure sine that holds the root of each chord under a rootless pad, and breathes with it: the same eight-bar filter tide, smaller. It should hide inside the pad, so you feel the chord change more than you hear a bass. From Low Tide, step 3, track 2. In step 4 the same instrument plays the bassline.

Play gives the whole Low Tide loop. Mute T3 to hear the roots alone. They play on a plain browser tone close to this shape.

Play it

One note per chord, on row 0, with an empty phrase after it so the note holds for two bars.

Why these values

  • SHAPE SINE TRIANGLE: a sine and a triangle sent into a wavefolder. COLOR 10 keeps it almost all sine; TIMBRE 00 doesn't fold it at all. Few harmonics, so nothing pokes out above the pad.
  • CUTOFF 50, RES 00, AMP 00: rounds off whatever is left.
  • DRY 80, REV 00: quiet and dry. Reverb on the low end turns to mud under a wet pad.
  • MOD 1 ADSR: a soft attack (about 0.5 s on the help line) so a root swells in from silence, and a 1 s release after an OFF. ADSR times are seconds; set them by the help line.
  • MOD 2 LFO on CUTOFF: the pad's tide (FREQ 80, eight bars) at AMT 20, so the floor darkens and opens with the pad.

Try

  • Still too present? Lower DRY first, then an EQ low shelf (Low Tide step 10).
  • TIMBRE 30 if you're listening on the M8's speaker and can't hear it at all; back to 00 on headphones.
  • Put the Tide pad's rootless banks over any root here, and the root names the chord.
