Patch card · Macrosynth · low roots

Roots

A soft, round low tone that holds the root of each chord so a rootless pad can float above it. Nothing about it should draw the ear; it's the floor. From Low Tide, step 3, track 2.

Play gives the whole Low Tide loop. Mute T3 to hear the roots alone. The roots play on a plain browser tone close to this shape; your M8 will be rounder.

Play it

One note per chord, on row 0, with an empty phrase after it so the note holds for two bars.

Why these values

  • SHAPE SINE TRIANGLE (model 03): a sine and a triangle sent into a wavefolder. COLOR 20 keeps it mostly sine; TIMBRE 18 folds it just enough to be heard on a small speaker.
  • CUTOFF 70, RES 10: rounds off whatever the folding adds.
  • DRY C0, REV 10: nearly dry. Reverb on the low end turns to mud under a wet pad.
  • No envelope: the note holds until the next one arrives, which is what a pedal root wants.

Try

  • TIMBRE 40 for a bass that speaks on a phone speaker; back to 18 on headphones.
  • COLOR 80 for an even sine and triangle: a little reedier.
  • Put the Tide pad's rootless banks over any root here, and the root names the chord.
