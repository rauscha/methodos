Polaroid arp

A hollow triangle pluck: a rounded "doong" that fades to an almost wooden tone, like a music box heard through a wall, with a tape wobble you feel more than hear. From the Faded Polaroid session, where it replaced the Glass Garden FM voice.

The browser plays the notes on a plain tone with no pluck. Mute T2 and T3 to hear the arp alone.

Play it

Eighth notes on the even rows, moving through each chord's upper notes rather than up a triad. Bar 1 is Dmaj9; the other three bars are phrases 11, 12 and 13.

Why these values

  • SHAPE TRIANGLE, WARP 18: a soft fundamental, pushed slightly to one side for a little asymmetry.
  • CUTOFF A0, RES 12: takes the top off; the bit of resonance adds the nasal edge.
  • AHD on volume: ATK 01, HOLD 01, DEC 18. At 90 BPM a tick is about 28 ms, so the attack is one tick and 18 (24 ticks) is one beat. The next note arrives after half a beat and takes over the track, so you hear the first half of each fade: a pluck, not a drone.
  • AHD on cutoff: AMT 20, DEC 10. A small brightness at the start of each note that closes in two-thirds of a beat. That's the "doong".
  • LFO on pitch: SIN, FREE, AMT 01, FREQ 80. FREQ counts steps, so 80 (128 steps) is one wobble every eight bars. AMT 01 is the smallest step; any more and it's seasick.
  • MFX 30, DEL 28, REV 48: the haze. Get it sounding right dry first.

Try

  • SHAPE SINE for something purer.
  • SIZE from 20 toward 50 for more grain.
  • A little more WARP.

This patch is the chat's recipe. Two values are fixed from it: CHO became MFX and PAN 00 became 80, which is center on 6.6.3. Is the parameter after WARP labeled SCAN or MIRROR on your screen?
