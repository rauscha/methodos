FM sub

A deep, round bass with a soft knock at the front of each note and no buzz. FM used the opposite way from the Glass keys: a modulator at the note's own frequency, and an envelope on it so fast you hear it as an attack, not a tone. Overpass's bass (step 8).

The browser plays the notes, slides and volume envelope on a plain tone; the FM is your M8's.

Why these values

  • ALGO 07 [A>B]+[C>D]: A bends B; C bends D. You hear B and D.
  • A at ratio 1.00, level 60, MOD slot 1 LEV: a modulator at the note's own frequency, so the bend stays harmonic and rubbery. Its level follows MOD1, which sits at 00.
  • C at level 00: off, so D is a plain sine, the clean fundamental under the rubber.
  • CUTOFF 70: the upper partials off. PAN 80, DRY D0, sends 00: mono, center, dry.
  • MOD 1, AHD on VOLUME: ATK 01, HOLD 05, DEC 06: an eighth note long, one tick of attack so the sine doesn't click.
  • MOD 2, AHD on MOD1: DEC 04: A's level from full to nothing in 4 ticks. That burst of harmonics is the knock.

Play it

Short notes, rests between them, an octave pop, and a PSL 03 slide into each new root an eighth early.

A held version: SUB HOLD

For long notes, copy it and change MOD 1 to an ADSR on VOLUME (ATK about 0.01 s, REL about 0.48 s, SUS FF, by the help line): it holds until the next note or an OFF. Overpass's B section uses it as 0B, and both carry a trig envelope that ducks them under the kick.

Try

  • MOD 2's DEC at 08: a longer knock, nearer a plucked string.
  • A little FB on A: grit.
