No-sample drum kit

Kick, snare and hats from three Macrosynth drum models, so a beat needs no SD card and no samples. The pattern is the one planned for Low Tide: kick on 1 and the "and" of 3, snare on 2 and 4 with a ghost at the end of the bar, eighth-note hats with the beat accented.

The kick (track 1)

  • SHAPE 22 KICK: an 808 bass drum circuit. TIMBRE is the decay, COLOR the tone (brightness). The note you type tunes it.
  • AMP 20, LIM CLIP: a little drive for punch.

The snare (track 2)

  • SHAPE 24 SNARE: the 808 snare. TIMBRE balances its two tones, COLOR is the noise ("snappy").
  • Row F, velocity 20: the ghost note. Quiet on purpose; it leads into the next bar.

The hats (track 3)

  • SHAPE 23 CYMBAL: raw cymbal material. COLOR balances metallic squares against noise; TIMBRE sets a band-pass filter's cutoff.
  • AHD on volume, DEC 06. The cymbal model drones, so the envelope makes the hit. 06 is six ticks, one row: a closed hat. Try 18 (a beat) for an open one.
  • HIGHPASS at 80: keeps the hats out of the kick's way.
  • Velocity 60 on the beat, 30 off it: the accents are the groove.

Swing

Low Tide swings its drums and bass but not its pad. Groove 00 is every track's default, so leave it alone and use groove 01. In the groove view (SHIFT+UP from the phrase), on groove 01, set rows 0 and 1 to 07 and 05: the first of each pair of rows gets 7 ticks, the second 5, still 12 together. Then put GRV 01 on row 0 of each drum phrase.

Values are a starting point, not tried on an M8. Do KICK and SNARE decay on their own, or do they need a volume envelope like the hats? Which note sounds right for the kick (D-2 here is a guess)?
