No-sample drum kit

Kick, snare and hats from three Macrosynth drum models, so a beat needs no SD card and no samples. This is Low Tide's beat (step 5): kick on 1 and the "and" of 3, snare on 2 and 4 with a ghost at the end of the bar, eighth-note hats with soft swung sixteenths between.

Kick and snare (track 1)

A track plays one note at a time; these two never land on the same row, so they share. Kick is instrument 06, snare 07.

  • SHAPE 22 KICK: an 808 bass drum circuit. TIMBRE is the decay, COLOR the tone (brightness). The note you type tunes it.
  • AMP 20, LIM CLIP: a little drive for punch.
  • SHAPE 24 SNARE: the 808 snare. TIMBRE balances its two tones, COLOR is the noise ("snappy").
  • Row F, velocity 20: the ghost note. Quiet on purpose; it leads into the next bar.

Hats (track 6)

  • SHAPE 23 CYMBAL: raw cymbal material. COLOR balances metallic squares against noise; TIMBRE sets a band-pass filter's cutoff.
  • AHD on volume, DEC 06. The cymbal model drones, so the envelope makes the hit. AHD counts ticks, so 06 is one row: a closed hat. Try 18 (a beat) for an open one.
  • HIGHPASS at 80: keeps the hats out of the kick's way.
  • Velocity 60 on the beat, 30 off it, 18 on rows 3 and B: the accents are the groove.

Swing

Groove 00 is every track's default, so leave it alone and use groove 01. In the groove view (SHIFT+UP from the phrase), on groove 01, set rows 0 and 1 to 07 and 05: the first of each pair of rows gets 7 ticks, the second 5, still 12 together. Then put GRV 01 on row 0 of the drum phrases. Only odd rows move, so the swing lives in the quiet hats on rows 3 and B and the snare ghost on F.

Ducking other parts

To make a pad or bass breathe on the kick, give it a trig envelope with SRC 06, the kick instrument. SRC 80 would mean all of track 1, snare included. See Low Tide, step 6.

Values are a starting point, not tried on an M8. Report back: does the kick at TIMBRE 60 die away on its own before beat 2?
