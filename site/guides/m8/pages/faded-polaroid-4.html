Faded Polaroid · step 4 of 10

The arp

Your arp from the session, unchanged: eighth notes through each chord's upper notes, on a Wavsynth triangle that plucks and fades. This step is about the sound, the Polaroid arp card, and about one thing the notes already do right: they sit on even rows.

Listen forEach note is a soft "doong" whose bright edge is gone almost at once, fading before the next eighth or two. Under it, very slowly, the pitch wobbles, like a stretched tape: you should barely notice it, and miss it if it stopped.

Why even rows matter

In step 5 a swing groove arrives on the drums. A swing groove changes how long each row of a pair lasts, so it moves only the odd rows (1, 3, 5 …) late. Every arp note is on an even row, so even if the arp's track used the groove, nothing in it would move. The arp stays straight while the drums lilt around it.

Instrument 01: Polaroid arp

  • SHAPE TRIANGLE, WARP 18: a soft, hollow fundamental, pushed slightly to one side: the woody asymmetry.
  • SCAN 00: the row after WARP. The card asked whether your screen says SCAN or MIRROR; the changelog settles it ("Wavsynth MIRROR parameter renamed SCAN").
  • CUTOFF A0, RES 12: never glassy; a small nasal edge.
  • MFX 30, DEL 28, REV 48: the haze. Get it sounding right dry first.

Three mods, all in ticks or steps

  • MOD 1, AHD on VOLUME: ATK 01, HOLD 01, DEC 18. One tick up, one tick held, then a fade over 24 ticks, one beat. The next note arrives half a beat later and takes over the track, so you hear the first half of each fade: a pluck, not a drone.
  • MOD 2, AHD on CUTOFF: AMT 20, DEC 10. A little brightness at the start of each note that closes in 16 ticks, two-thirds of a beat. That's the "doong".
  • MOD 3, LFO on PITCH: SIN, FREE, AMT 01, FREQ 80. FREQ is a length: 80 reads "8 PHRASES", so one wobble every eight bars. AMT 01 is the smallest step. Any more and it's seasick.

The demo plays the pluck's volume shape; it doesn't bend the pitch or move the filter.

Things to try

  • DEC 0C instead of 18: drier, more like a music box.
  • SIZE from 20 toward 50 for more grain.
  • Mute track 3 and play the arp over the bass alone: the chords are all there in the arp.
