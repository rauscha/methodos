Low Tide · step 9 of 10

Arrange it in the song view

So far everything lives in song row 00, one eight-bar loop. Now the song view lays out a whole piece, eight bars per row: an intro, the parts arriving one at a time, a breakdown where the filter closes, the full loop again, and an outro where the pad lets go. About two and a half minutes.

RowWhat happens
00Intro: the pad swells in over the texture.
01The bass enters.
02Drums and hats enter; the pad starts to breathe.
03The arp: everything.
04Breakdown. No drums, no bass; the mixer's filter closes over the rest and opens again in bar 8.
05Everything again.
06Outro: the pad holds Dm9 and fades; the texture carries on alone.
Press Play and let it run. The transport reads the song row as it goes. Notice row 04: when the drums leave, the pad stops ducking, and the filter closing makes the room sound smaller. Then the drop back in on row 05.

A chain for silence: FE

A track that should be quiet for a row can't just be left empty. In the song view an empty cell (--) ends that track's list, and it would loop back to its top out of step with the others. The manual's answer is a chain of empty phrases; FE is the usual number. This one starts with an OFF, because an empty phrase on its own lets the last note keep ringing.

  1. On an empty song cell, EDIT, then dial the chain number to FE with EDIT+arrows (EDIT+UP/DOWN moves in big steps).
  2. In chain FE: phrase FD on row 0, phrase FE on rows 1 to 7.
  3. Phrase FD, row 0: an OFF. On the empty note (---), OPTION+EDIT inserts one. Phrase FE stays empty.

Lay out the rows

  1. Copy row 00 down to rows 03 and 05 first, while it's still the full loop: on row 00, track 1, SHIFT+OPTION starts a selection; move right to track 6 to stretch it; OPTION copies. Put the cursor on row 03, track 1, and SHIFT+EDIT pastes. Same again on row 05.
  2. Then fill the rest from the song screen above. Cells that read FE are the silence chain. Edit row 00 last: it becomes the intro.
  3. Track 8 gets FE on every row except 04, so it stays in step too.

Each track's song list must be as long as the others, or they drift apart: here every row is eight bars on every track, and the empty row 07 sends them all back to row 00 together.

The outro: chain 33

The pad gets its own last chain: Dm9 struck again and held, the top E for a bar, Dm9 again, then an OFF in bar 7 so the two-bar release ends with the row.

Make it by cloning: on chain 03 in row 06, SHIFT+OPTION then EDIT copies it to a new, unused chain number. These pages call that chain 33; yours may get another number, which is fine. Then change its rows. Phrase 3E holds the OFF.

The breakdown: track 8 moves the DJ filter

Track 8 plays no notes. Its phrases only carry DJC, which sets the cutoff of the mixer's DJ filter. The filter sits on the whole mix: 80 is off, below 80 it's a low-pass that closes as the value falls.

  1. In the Mixer view, put the cursor on DJF and tap EDIT. Since 6.6.1 that "captures" its command, so tapping EDIT on an empty FX column in a phrase inserts DJC. (Or pick DJC from the FX help list.)
  2. Chain 80 on track 8, row 04: phrases 80, 8F, 81, 8F, 82, 8F, 8F, 83.
  3. Phrases 80, 81, 82: one DJC on row 0, at 60, 40 and 30. 8F is empty: the filter stays where it was put.
  4. Phrase 83, bar 8, opens it back up a beat at a time: 48, 58, 68, then 80 on beat 4, just before the drums return.

The DJ filter is a mixer setting, so it stays where the last DJC left it. If you stop in the middle of the breakdown, the next play starts muffled: set DJF back to 80 in the mixer.

Playing it

  • PLAY in the song view plays from the cursor's row. Put it on row 00 for the whole song.
  • While it plays, LEFT+PLAY on a row cues it next, which is the quick way to hear the breakdown into row 05 again and again.
  • Since 6.6, pressing LEFT twice on track 1 selects a whole song row; then EDIT+UP/DOWN moves it, if you want to try another order.

Things to try

  • A second breakdown chain for the pad with the arp left out, so row 04 thins even further.
  • Keep the arp in the outro: track 4, row 06, chain 04 instead of FE.
  • Swap the order of rows 01 and 02, so the drums arrive before the bass.
