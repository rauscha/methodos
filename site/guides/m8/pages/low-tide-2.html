The pad sound

Before writing any chords, make the sound on one chord and hold it. Shaping a pad is about time: how slowly it arrives, how long it hangs after you let go, and how it changes while it's held. That's easier to judge on one chord than on eight bars of them.

One chord in phrase 30

  1. In the Song view, put the cursor on track 3, row 00, and press EDIT to make a chain. Set it to 03 with EDIT+arrows.
  2. SHIFT+RIGHT into the chain. On row 0, make phrase 30.
  3. SHIFT+RIGHT into the phrase. On row 0 enter the note F-3 and set the I column to 03.

Nothing else in the phrase. With no OFF, the note keeps sounding, and because the chain is one phrase long, it plays again every bar.

Which octave the M8 means by F-3. The pages and the demo assume C-4 is middle C (261 Hz). The manual calls C-1 the lowest octave, which could put everything one or two octaves lower than the demo plays it. Report back: play C-4 on any instrument against a tuner app. Is it middle C?

Instrument 03: Hypersynth

SHIFT+RIGHT from the phrase into instrument 03 and set TYPE to HYPERSYNTH. It's a chord synth: up to six notes per chord, each one a pair of detuned saws, from 16 chord banks you program yourself.

SettingWhy
SHAPE SOFT SAWNew in 6.6. Fewer bright harmonics than a plain saw, so the pad is warm before the filter touches it. Try DARK SAW for murkier.
CHORD 00Bank 00 holds the intervals 00 04 07 0B: on F-3 that's F A C E, the upper part of Dm9. The other banks come in step 3.
SWARM 30Detunes each note's two saws. At 30 it's a slow shimmer, not a chorus wobble.
WIDTH C0Spreads the notes across the stereo field, which is most of what makes a pad sound big on headphones.
SUBOSC 00Off. The roots track will carry the bottom, and a sub here would double it.
CUTOFF 80, RES 20Lowpass, half closed. The LFO below will move it either side of here.
DRY B0, REV 90A lot of reverb for the M8. A pad can take it; the bass and drums won't.

How the CHORD banks are edited on the screen (choosing a bank, then entering its intervals), and whether the intervals are semitones in hex as written here. The manual describes CHORD only as "the chord bank and intervals for the selected bank". Report back: one photo of the CHORD field while editing a bank.

Make it breathe

SHIFT+UP from the instrument opens its four modulation slots.

MOD 1: a slow swell

An ADSR on VOLUME. The M8 counts envelope time in ticks, 24 to a quarter note, so 96 ticks is one bar at any tempo. ATK 60 is 96 ticks: the pad takes a full bar to arrive. REL C0 is 192 ticks: it takes two bars to fade after an OFF.

An ADSR only releases on an OFF (a note OFF or the OFF command). This phrase has none, so while it plays, the pad just holds at the top. Press Stop on the strip to send an OFF and watch the two-bar release.

The phrase retriggers the chord every bar. Since 6.6, a retrigger of the same instrument resumes the attack from wherever the envelope already is, so a held pad doesn't dip each bar. That matters for step 3, where every chord change is a retrigger.

MOD 2: the tide

An LFO on CUTOFF: OSC SIN for a smooth sweep, TRIG FREE so it keeps running across notes instead of restarting on each one, AMT 40 for a modest sweep, and FREQ 80, meant to be one slow cycle every eight bars.

Which way FREQ goes. The manual calls it "the rate at which the LFO cycles, represented in steps", so a higher value may well be faster, not slower, and the demo above assumes the opposite. Report back: set FREQ to 04, then 80. Which one sweeps faster? Then find the value that takes about eight bars. The octave-wide sweep drawn above is the demo's own scale for AMT, not an M8 fact.

Press PLAY in the phrase view and wait. The first chord takes a full bar to arrive. Then, over the next eight bars, the tone darkens until it's almost muffled and slowly opens up again. That's the tide. When it's at its darkest, the reverb is most of what you hear.

Things to try

  • SWARM 60: more motion. Past about 80 it starts to sound out of tune.
  • LFO OSC TRI: the sweep turns at the ends instead of lingering.
  • ATK 30: half a bar. Quicker, less dreamlike.
  • Press PLAY in the instrument view, then EDIT+LEFT/RIGHT on CUTOFF, to hear by hand what the LFO is doing for you.
