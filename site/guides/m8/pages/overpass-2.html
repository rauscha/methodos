One FM voice

The stabs start as an electric piano made on the FM synth: a glassy tine at the front of each note, gone within a beat, over a round body that rings out over the bar. It's the Glass keys card, Glass Garden's second chance, with one value changed. This step builds it and explains what each part of an FM patch does, since FM is the M8's hardest instrument.

The demo plays the notes and the envelope on a plain tone. FM is your M8's.

How the FM synth works

Four operators, A to D, each a simple wave. In FM, one operator (a modulator) bends the pitch of another (a carrier) so fast that you don't hear a bend: you hear new harmonics. You only hear the carriers. The ALGO decides which operator bends which; there are 12 (manual, FM Synth).

  • RATIO: each operator's frequency relative to the note. 2.00 is an octave up, 0.50 an octave down.
  • LEV and FB: its level, and how much it feeds back into itself. On a modulator, more level means more harmonics: brighter. On a carrier, it's just louder.
  • MOD: two slots per operator. Each assigns one of the four MOD1–MOD4 values to one of the operator's settings. LEV "controls the level from 00 to the currently set level" (manual); RAT, PIT and FBK do the same for ratio, pitch and feedback.
  • MOD1 to MOD4 are the instrument's own rows. Point a mod slot (an envelope, an LFO) at one of them, and through the operator's MOD slot it moves that operator.

Instrument 0A: Glass keys

  1. Instrument 0A: TYPE FMSYNTH, NAME GLASS KEYS.
  2. ALGO 07, which draws as [A>B]+[C>D]: two pairs side by side. A bends B, C bends D, and you hear B and D.
  3. The four operator rows as below. With the cursor in an operator's row, SHIFT+OPTION copies it and SHIFT+EDIT pastes it on another, which saves typing.
  4. MOD1 00, CUTOFF FF, DRY C0, and the sends MFX, DEL, REV all 00.
  • A into B, both at ratio 1.00, A at level 30: a gentle bend of B, the round body you hear. More A level makes it reedier.
  • C at ratio 14.00 into D at 1.00: a modulator fourteen times the note's frequency puts a cluster of high, slightly bell-like partials on D. That's the glass.
  • C's MOD slot set to 1 LEV: C's level follows MOD1. At MOD1 00, C is silent until something raises MOD1.
  • The sends at 00: this instrument is only ever heard rendered (step 4), and a dry render makes clean stabs. The stab instruments add their own space.

Two envelopes: the body and the tine

SHIFT+UP from the instrument opens its mod slots.

  • MOD 1, AHD on VOLUME: AMT FF, ATK 00, HOLD 00, DEC 60. The whole note fades over 96 ticks, one bar (1.94 s at 124 BPM): it rings out.
  • MOD 2, AHD on MOD1: AMT FF, ATK 00, HOLD 00, DEC 0C. It lifts MOD1 to full and lets it fall to nothing in 12 ticks, an eighth (0.24 s). Through C's 1 LEV slot, the glass is only on the attack, as on a real tine.

The one change from the card: MOD1 FF to 00

The card had MOD1 at FF. An M8 mod adds to the value it moves (as the arp's cutoff envelope adds to its CUTOFF), so with MOD1 already at full, the envelope on it has nowhere to go and the tine never fades. At 00, the envelope takes C from full to nothing in an eighth. The card is updated to match.

How an FM mod slot reaches an operator. The card's routing (MOD 2's DEST set to MOD1, and C's MOD slot to 1 LEV) is the reading of the manual, which says what the operator slots do but not how a mod slot's DEST names them. Report back: what DEST choices does a mod slot show on an FM instrument?

MOD1 00 as the base. If the tine is silent, MOD1 at 00 may be holding C shut: raise MOD1 a little until it speaks. Report back: does the tine fade in an eighth?

Listen forPreview it with EDIT+PLAY in the instrument view, on a C. A bright "tink" right at the front, gone before you can name it, then a round, soft note that dies away over about two seconds. If you hear glass all the way through, the envelope isn't reaching C. Then flip ALGO to 0B (A+B+C+D: every operator heard, none bending another) and back, to hear the difference FM makes.

Things to try

  • C's ratio at 7.00 or 3.50: a different glass.
  • MOD 2's DEC at 18, a beat: a longer shimmer.
  • A little FB on A: a dirtier, harder body.
