Why these values

  • SHAPE 28 WAV PARAPHONIC: four wavetable voices. TIMBRE moves through the waveforms; COLOR chooses one of 16 chord shapes, one per first hex digit (00–0F, 10–1F, and so on up to F0–FF).
  • COLOR 80 as the base. COL is relative: it adds to COLOR, and every new note with an instrument number resets COLOR to 80 first. So each bar's command is "80 plus this": COL B4 takes 4C off (80 → 34), COL 24 adds 24 (80 → A4), COL 44 adds 44 (80 → C4).
  • Land on x4, not x8. Inside each region the shape morphs toward the next one. You found x0–x4 clean and x8 "gnarly". 34, A4 and C4 sit in the clean part.
  • Rootless. Four voices can't hold a five-note ninth chord, so the chord leaves out the root and Velvet bass plays it. F♯ minor 7 over a D is Dmaj9.
  • AHD on volume: ATK 02, HOLD 60, DEC 60. 60 is 96 ticks, one bar. The chord holds at full for exactly a bar, so each one stays full until the next takes over, and the last chord of a section fades out over a bar.

Try

  • Swap bar 2 for the softer iii: Imaj9, iii7, vi9, IVmaj9 (Dmaj9, F♯m7, Bm9, Gmaj9). F♯m7 is the same shape as bar 1, so bar 2 becomes A3 with COL B4, over an F♯ bass.
  • An x8 value on purpose, on the last bar only, for a smeared turnaround.
  • A slow LFO on TIMBRE so the chord's tone drifts while its shape stays put.
