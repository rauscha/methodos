How these pages work

Every page in this guide is something to read with the M8 in your hands. Some pages are mostly text. The ones that build a song also have a strip across the top that plays the song so far, and pictures that move with it.

The strip at the top

Play starts the song from the top and loops it. Stop sends every note an OFF, so you can watch and hear the releases ring out. Each track has a button that mutes it; a muted track is struck through and drawn with a dashed outline. The strip stays at the top while you scroll.

The sound is a stand-in made in the browser, not the M8. It plays the same notes, envelopes and filter moves the page tells you to enter, so it's right about what happens and when. Your M8 will sound richer.

The pictures

  • Copies of the M8's screens (phrase, chain, instrument) show exactly what to type. A row or value marked NEW, with a bar in the left margin, is what this step adds. While the song plays, a ▶ cursor and bold type mark the row the M8 would be on.
  • The piano roll shows every track as notes on a keyboard, with the chord names along the top. The pad's notes are outlined and hatched, the bass is solid, and the pad's top voice is drawn as a thick line, so you can follow the melody hidden in the chords.
  • The progression shows each chord with its Roman numeral. The chord sounding now is filled dark and marked "now"; the next one has a thick outline and says "next". Each card says how the harmony moves on.
  • Envelopes draw the shape, with the note's length underneath and a mark where the OFF arrives. A dot rides the shape as the song plays.
  • Filters show the cutoff moving over time, and the filter's curve laid over what the track is actually playing.

Things marked unverified

A dashed box like this one means something Claude couldn't confirm in the manual for firmware 6.6.3: a button press, a parameter's units, a command's name. Try it on the M8 and say what you found, and the page gets fixed.

Report back

Three things only you can check. A text with the answers, and a photo or two, fixes the pages.

  1. On a Hypersynth phrase, EDIT+UP on FX1: photo of the list. Is there a chord-bank command? (step 3)
  2. How a CHORD bank is picked and its intervals entered: one photo. (step 2)
  3. After pressing Play on step 2: did any picture tell you something the words didn't? Which one?

The octave the M8 means by "F-3", and which way LFO FREQ runs, are noted on step 2 but not worth checking: use standard notation and shift on the device as needed.

The manual on hand is for 6.5.2, and the 6.6 changes come from Dirtywave's changelog. Your own tests win over both: the Faded Polaroid session found that COL is relative by trying it.
