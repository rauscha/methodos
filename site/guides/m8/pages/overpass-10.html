Arrange and mix

Nine rows of eight bars, about two and a half minutes, arranged the way a beat-led track is: parts arriving and leaving in whole rows, a half-time B section, a breakdown that strips back and builds into the drop, a dub where the voice is thrown into the delay. Then the mix, with the low end made mono under the wide stabs.

The silence chain: FE in every gap

As in Faded Polaroid: an empty song cell sends its track back to the start of its list (manual), so every gap holds chain FE: phrase FD (an OFF on row 0), then the empty FE seven times. Make it once; it goes in every cell the song screen shows as FE.

The B section goes half-time, and the bass holds

In row 04 the kick plays only beat 1 and the clap only beat 3: the tempo stays, the feel halves. The bass holds F2 for two bars, slides up to G2 (PSL 06), and so on, ending with an OFF (phrase 4C). Its short notes would leave holes, so it's played on 0B SUB HOLD: 04 copied, with an ADSR that sustains.

  • Make it: on 04, SHIFT+OPTION; OPTION+RIGHT to 0B; SHIFT+EDIT. In its mod view, change MOD 1's type to 01 ADSR ENV.
  • ATK until the help line reads about 0.01 s; REL about 0.48 s, a beat at 124 BPM; SUS FF. It holds until the next note or the OFF, then lets go over a beat.

The duck: the bass gets out of the kick's way

On 04 and 0B, MOD 3 is a trig envelope on VOLUME, fired by track 1: SRC 80 is track 1 (above 7F, SRC counts tracks; Dirtywave meetups). AMT negative, about 40%; ATK 00, HOLD 01, DEC 06. Each kick pulls the bass down for a moment, so the two don't pile up on beat 1. It's Low Tide's pad duck, on a bass.

How a negative trig AMT reads on screen. "About 40%, negative" is the target; set it by ear. Report back: how does a negative AMT read?

The breakdown and the build

Row 05: no kick, hats or bass. The stabs drop to one a bar (chain 52), the voice sings its first bar once (chain 62), and the clap is silent for six bars, then plays the build from step 6 (chain 22). Track 8 closes the DJ filter and opens it, a beat at a time, into the drop.

DJC sets the cutoff of the mixer's DJ filter: 80 is off, below 80 it closes. It stays where the last DJC left it, so playback started from a middle row keeps whatever the last one set (the outro leaves it at 20, nearly shut). If the song sounds muffled, set DJF back to 80 in the mixer.

The peak and the dub

  • Row 06, chain 63: the hook, but its last note becomes F♯ (phrase 63): the voice joins the Em9 stab's bright note.
  • Row 07, chain 64: stabs and rain gone. The A of bar 1 and the C of bar 2 are thrown into the delay on 0C VOICE DUB: 06 copied, with DEL C0 (from 18) and REV 30 (from 40), so the throws repeat in the delay rather than wash out in the reverb. Then gaps where the line used to continue.

The last stab: DE1

The outro ends on one Am9 that rings out (phrase 5D). 05's own envelope is a short stab, so the note carries DE1 60: DE[1–2] offsets an envelope's decay for that note (manual, Instrument FX Commands). 10 + 60 = 70, 112 ticks, a little over a bar.

That the "1" in DE1 is mod slot 1. If the last stab stays short, use a copy of 05 with DEC 60 for that one note. Report back: does the last stab ring for about a bar?

The rain

Track 7, instrument 07 RAIN: Low Tide's FIELD loop (or a new rain recording), set duller for this song. Triggered once in row 00 (chain 70), carried through rows 01 to 06 by chain 71, which has no notes, stopped by the OFF in chain 72 for the dub, and started again in row 08.

  • TRANSP. OFF: a texture nothing should move. PLAY FWDLOOP: it loops as long as the note holds.
  • LOWPASS, CUTOFF 70: wet, not crisp. Low Tide's card used HIGHPASS 30 against rumble; here EQ 07's low cut does that job (below).
  • DRY 58, REV 50: quieter than the hats, far back.
  • MOD 1, ADSR on VOLUME: ATK about 0.5 s, REL about 1.0 s, SUS FF, by the help line. It fades in when it starts and fades out over a second at the OFF, instead of cutting.

That a held FWDLOOP sample keeps playing through rows whose chain has no note (chain 71). If the rain stops at row 01, put chain 70 in every rain row, as Low Tide does. Report back: does the rain carry through rows 01 to 06?

The mix

From the Mixer view, SHIFT+DOWN opens the effect settings. Starting points by ear.

  • Reverb SIZE A0, DECAY 90: a street under an overpass, big but not a hall, and short enough that the beat stays clear. SHIMMER 00: this song's lift is the stabs, not the tail.
  • Delay TIME 0C, FEEDBACK 80: a dotted eighth, so each throw lands off the beat and repeats through a bar or more. Its INPUT EQ cuts both ends, about 300 Hz and 4 kHz: darker, thinner repeats, the dub sound. The stabs' small send (DEL 10) keeps their echoes faint.
  • ModFX CHORUS: the width the mono stabs get.

That delay TIME 0C is a dotted eighth (Low Tide step 7 asks too). Report back: what does the help line say for 0C?

A mono low end: the main mix EQ

Mixer view, EDIT on EQ: LOW band, TYPE LOWCUT, MODE SIDE, about 150 Hz. The low end of everything (kick, bass, the reverb's low tail) goes mono; above it the chorus and reverb stay wide. Low Tide used the same trick on one instrument at about 500 Hz (Dirtywave's meetup #32); on the whole mix it wants to be much lower.

  • EQ 05 on the stabs (05, 08): a low cut at about 150 Hz. Their lowest note is A3, 220 Hz; below that belongs to the bass.
  • EQ 06 on the voice (06, 09, 0C): about 200 Hz, mic rumble and pops out.
  • EQ 07 on the rain: about 200 Hz, traffic rumble out from under the bass.
  • LIM about 30, raised until the limiter's line on the MIX meter moves only on the drops into rows 02 and 06. OTT 00. Balance with each instrument's DRY.

The ADSR targets. The help line reads seconds; the hex for each isn't known. Report back: which hex values give 0.010 and 0.480 s?
