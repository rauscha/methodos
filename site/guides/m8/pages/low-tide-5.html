Low Tide · step 5 of 10

Drums and swing

A slow trip-hop beat from three Macrosynth drum models, so it needs no samples: the No-sample drum kit card. Kick and snare share track 1; the hats get track 6. Then a groove swings the drums and the bass, and leaves the pad straight.

The demo plays no drums: a browser tone would only misrepresent them. Their phrases below still light up as the song plays, and the bass now swings.

The beat

PartRowsCount
Kick0, A1, and the "and" of 3
Snare4, C, ghost on F2 and 4, a quiet one leading into the next bar
Hatsevery even row, ghosts on 3 and Beighths, beats loud and offbeats soft

Track 1: kick and snare

An M8 track plays one note at a time, and these two never land on the same row, so they can share. Kick is instrument 06, snare 07.

  1. Song view, track 1, row 00: chain 01. In it, phrase 10 on rows 0 to 6 and 11 on row 7.
  2. Phrase 10: the rows below. Notes on instrument 06 are kicks, on 07 snares.
  3. Phrase 11 is bar 8: the same, plus snare ghosts on D, E and F that build into the top of the loop. Clone phrase 10 to start it: on its number in the chain, SHIFT+OPTION then EDIT.

Track 6: hats

Instrument 08 on every row of chain 06, phrase 60 eight times.

The three instruments

Set them up as on the drum kit card, which explains each value:

Inst.Settings
06 KICKMACROSYN, SHAPE 22 KICK, TIMBRE 60, COLOR 30, AMP 20, DRY E0
07 SNAREMACROSYN, SHAPE 24 SNARE, TIMBRE 40, COLOR 90, AMP 10, DRY C0, REV 30
08 HATMACROSYN, SHAPE 23 CYMBAL, TIMBRE C0, COLOR E0, FILTER HIGHPASS at 80, DRY A0, REV 18; MOD 1 AHD ENV on VOLUME, DEC 06

The hat's AHD is in ticks (you found AHD ATK 10 reads "2 STEPS 4 TICKS", 6 ticks to a step), so DEC 06 is exactly one row: a closed hat.

The kick's length. The manual says KICK's TIMBRE is its decay, which suggests it dies away on its own; if it doesn't, the snare on row 4 cuts it anyway. Report back: at TIMBRE 60, does the kick stop by itself before beat 2?

Swing: groove 01

A groove sets how many ticks each row lasts. Groove 00, the default for every track, gives every row 6. Swing makes the first row of each pair longer and the second shorter, so every odd row arrives late.

  1. From any phrase, SHIFT+UP opens the Groove view. OPTION+RIGHT moves to groove 01, so 00 stays straight.
  2. Set row 0 to 07 with EDIT+UP/DOWN: that changes row 1 with it, to 05. Rows 2 to F stay empty, so the pair repeats. 7 + 5 = 12, the same as 6 + 6, so a phrase is still 96 ticks.
  3. Give each swung track groove 01 with GRV 01: on row 0 of phrase 10 (FX1), phrase 60 (FX1) and the bass's phrase 24 (FX2, beside the PSL). A GRV holds until another GRV, so once per track is enough.

Groove 07/05 swings sixteenths. Only the odd rows move, one tick late, and the even rows stay on the beat. That's why the hats have quiet notes on rows 3 and B and the bass has its ghost on row B: without notes on odd rows, a swing groove has nothing to move. The pad's only row is row 0, and it has no GRV, so it stays straight.

On the M8, mute track 6 (OPTION+SHIFT on it) and back: the ghosts on 3 and B are where the lilt is. In the demo, listen to the bass's row B ghost: it now arrives a little late, leaning into beat 4.

Things to try

  • 08 04 in groove 01 (the manual's other swing): heavier, closer to a shuffle.
  • 06 06 to hear the beat straight again, then back to 07 05.
  • Hat V on the offbeats from 30 down to 20: the accents become the groove.
