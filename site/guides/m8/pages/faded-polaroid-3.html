The bass

The chords leave their roots out, so the bass puts them back: one note a bar, held, on your Velvet bass. It's the floor the rootless shapes stand on. In step 5 it grows a small figure; for now it's roots alone.

Listen forMute T2 on the stripMute track 2 (OPTION+SHIFT on it) and bar 1 sounds like F♯ minor, not D major. Unmute it and it's Dmaj9 again. That's what a rootless chord is. Then follow the bass alone: down a fourth, up a step, down a third, and up a fifth home.

Instrument 02: Velvet bass

A Wavsynth. Set it exactly as on the card.

  • SHAPE SAW, CUTOFF 60, RES 08: a saw with its top shaved off. Full harmonics low down, nothing fizzy: the felt.
  • AMP 18, LIM POST: a little gain, clipped after the filter, so it thickens without getting brighter.
  • DRY D0, REV 08: loud enough to be the floor, almost dry. Reverb on the bass would muddy the chords above it.

Three beats long

The AHD on VOLUME is in ticks: ATK 01 (one tick, so no click), HOLD 18 (a beat), DEC 30 (two beats). Each root holds a beat and dies away over two more, so the bass breathes in each bar instead of droning. Because the chords hold for a bar, the last beat of each bar is chord alone.

The M8 has no B♭ or E♭ to type; every flat is entered as a sharp. This song only needs that in step 8.

Things to try

  • HOLD 30: the roots last the whole bar, and the song gets heavier.
  • SHAPE TRIANGLE: rounder still, and quieter on the M8's speaker.
