Polaroid lead

A breathy, airy lead, a recorder or wooden flute on a worn cassette: a little air and a small scoop at the start of each note, short notes plain, long notes growing a vibrato after about a beat. Faded Polaroid's hook (step 6, and its table in step 7).

The browser plays the notes, slides and the table's breath on a plain tone. Mute T3 to hear the lead alone.

Play it

The hook's first half, over the chords: a beat of rest, A5 and D6 eighths, E6 held into bar 2, then C♯6 and B5, which rests on the chord's ninth.

Its table

TBL. TIC 03: three ticks a row. Rows 0–2 scoop the note up into pitch in a sixteenth (FIN is relative, so F0, 08, 08 come back to zero) while V swells it; rows 3–7 wait; a beat in, rows 8–9 start a vibrato (PVB, speed then depth) and HOP 09 holds it there.

A second table, 81, picked with TBL 81, has a deeper scoop and a wider vibrato, for the hook's second telling.

Why these values

  • SHAPE 1F FLUTED: the BLOWN / FLUTED physical model. TIMBRE is air pressure, COLOR the instrument's geometry, and it has no filter of its own (manual).
  • TIMBRE 60, COLOR 40: enough pressure to speak on an eighth note; a short, wooden bore.
  • DEGRADE 08, CUTOFF A8: cassette grain, a soft top.
  • ADSR on VOLUME, ATK about 0.030 s and REL about 0.250 s by the help line, SUS FF: it holds while the note does and lets go quickly on an OFF. Since 6.6 a retrigger resumes from the current level, so tied notes join into one breath.
  • AHD on TIMBRE, AMT 18, DEC 06: a puff of extra air for one sixteenth.
  • LFO on PITCH, AMT 01, FREQ 80: one slow wobble every eight bars, the tape.
  • DRY B0, DEL 30, REV 70: soft, with dotted-eighth echoes and a long tail.

Try

  • TIMBRE 80: more air, nearer to overblowing.
  • 1E BLOWN, the reed model beside it: a reedier voice for the same line.
