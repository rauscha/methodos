The FM bass

A second FM patch, for the low end: deep and round, with a soft knock at the front of each note and no buzz. Short notes with rests between them, a pop up an octave, and a slide into every new chord an eighth early. It's FM used the other way from the Glass keys: low ratios and a very fast envelope. The card is FM sub.

Listen forEach note is short, about an eighth, with a soft "thock" right at the front. The rests are half the line. At the end of every second bar the bass slides into the next root early, and the stabs arrive with it: the loop leans forward. And now you can hear whether the stabs are in tune with it.

Instrument 04: FM sub

The same routing as the Glass keys, ALGO 07 [A>B]+[C>D], so this reads like step 2: one FM pair, and one plain sine.

  • A into B, both at ratio 1.00: a modulator at the note's own frequency bends B into a rubbery body, and the low ratio keeps the bend harmonic. A's MOD slot is 1 LEV: its level follows MOD1, which starts at 00.
  • C at level 00: off, so D is a plain sine at the note, the clean, deep fundamental under the rubber.
  • CUTOFF 70: takes the FM's upper partials off. No buzz.
  • PAN 80, DRY D0, sends 00: mono, dead center, dry. The floor.

The knock

  • MOD 1, AHD on VOLUME: ATK 01, HOLD 05, DEC 06. 12 ticks, an eighth (0.24 s). One tick of attack keeps the sine from clicking.
  • MOD 2, AHD on MOD1: AMT FF, ATK 00, HOLD 00, DEC 04. A's level from full to nothing in 4 ticks, 80 ms: a burst of harmonics at the front of each note, then the plain rubbery tone. That's the knock. The Glass keys' tine took 12 ticks; a bass wants it shorter.

Step 10 adds a third mod, a trig envelope that ducks the bass under the kick.

Things to try

  • MOD 2's DEC at 08: a longer, more obvious knock, closer to a plucked string.
  • A's ratio at 2.00, an octave up: hear how the body changes.
  • PSL 06 on the slides: lazier, more dub.
