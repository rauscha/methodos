Overpass · step 4 of 10

Render the chords

Now the M8 records its own four tracks into a sample: resampling. One render per chord, each cropped, normalized, made mono and given a short fade, then moved into its own Sampler instrument. After this, the four FM tracks are cleared, and the whole chord is one note on one track. That's polyphony from a mono tracker, and the start of chord memory. Sampling has the background.

Render each chord

For row 00 (Cm9), then again for row 01 (Cmaj9):

  1. Select the four cells. Song view, cursor on track 5 of the row. SHIFT+OPTION starts a selection; RIGHT three times covers tracks 5 to 8.
  2. Render. Double-tap EDIT; choose QUICK RENDER. Both bars of each chain, the chord and its tail, are rendered.
  3. A new Sampler instrument holding the render appears and becomes the default for new notes. In it, EDIT on EDIT opens the sample editor.
  4. Crop to the chord. On SELECT, start at the beginning; move the end with OPTION+UP/DOWN (beats at the song's tempo) to 4 beats: by then 0A's one-bar fade has finished. PROCESS CROP.
  5. NORMALIZE, with the whole sample selected. Quick renders are already 16-bit (changelog: "32-bit normalization process before finalizing to 16-bit").
  6. MONO: MIX. The four voices are all centered and dry, so nothing is lost, and a mono file halves what the pitch limit has to stream: the stabs play up to E-4, four semitones above C-4.
  7. A short fade at the end: SELECT the last beat, PROCESS the fade-out (FADE IN/OUT).
  8. NAME CM9 (for row 01: CMAJ9). SAVE. OPTION leaves.

Into 05 and 08

  1. On the CM9 instrument, SHIFT+OPTION copies it; OPTION+LEFT/RIGHT to 05; SHIFT+EDIT pastes. Set 05 as below.
  2. On 05, SHIFT+OPTION; go to 08; SHIFT+EDIT. On 08's SAMPLE, EDIT+LEFT/RIGHT on the name steps through the folder to CMAJ9.
  3. Clear the two slots the renders landed in (Instrument Pool view, EDIT+OPTION on each name), and clear tracks 5 to 8 of rows 00 and 01 (EDIT+OPTION on each cell). Those tracks and rows take the song's parts from step 5.

Instruments 05 and 08

  • SAMPLE CM9 on 05, CMAJ9 on 08. Both play once (PLAY FWD); the crop put the hit at the start.
  • TRANSP. ON, DETUNE 80: nothing transposes them, and in tune, so the typed note names the chord.
  • TBL. TIC 00: the table in step 7 moves one row per stab. Set it now.
  • DEGRADE 10, LOWPASS, CUTOFF B0, RES 10: a little worn, the tine softened. Round, not glassy.
  • DRY C0, MFX 18, DEL 10, REV 38 on 05; 08 is the same with REV 50, further in. The chorus gives the mono render width.
  • EQ 05 on both: a low cut, set up in step 10.

Their envelopes come in step 5, when you first play them.

Listen forIn 05, EDIT+PLAY previews it: the whole Cm9 from one key, tine and all. It should sound like row 00 did, a little duller. Then 08: the same, brighter.

Things to try

  • Before you clear tracks 5 to 8, render Cm9 again with 0A's MOD 2 DEC at 18 (a longer shimmer), name it CM9B, and compare the two in 05 by swapping SAMPLE.
