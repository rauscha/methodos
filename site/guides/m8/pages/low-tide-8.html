Low Tide · step 8 of 10

Texture: record a sound where you are

The last part is a sound from wherever you are: rain, a café, a train, an airport hum. Recorded on the M8's own mic, looped, and set low and far away under everything. It needs no computer. The instrument is the Field recording loop card, instrument 0B on track 5. For more on recording and samples, see Sampling.

The demo can't play your recording, so track 5 is silent here. Its chain and phrase still light up.

Record it

  1. Stop the song first, or the mic records the M8's own speaker.
  2. Go to instrument 0B (in phrase 50's I column, enter 0B, then SHIFT+RIGHT). Set TYPE to SAMPLER. Press EDIT on REC. to open the sample editor.
  3. Set SRC to MIC. Bring VOL up until the loudest part sits high without clipping.
  4. Start with the power button rather than EDIT on START, so the key click isn't in the recording. Hold still and record half a minute or more.
  5. SELECT a calm stretch of 5 to 10 seconds where no one sound sticks out (a voice, a door). Then PROCESS, choosing each with EDIT+LEFT/RIGHT and tapping EDIT to run it: CROP, then XFADE LOOP so the end blends into the start, then NORMALIZE.
  6. NAME it and SAVE. OPTION leaves the editor.

How a recording stops: the manual says how to start one (EDIT on START, or the power button) but not how to end it. Try the power button again. Report back: what stopped it?

The instrument

  • PLAY FWDLOOP: loops forward for as long as the note holds.
  • FILTER HIGHPASS, CUTOFF 30: takes out the rumble (traffic, wind, handling) that would fight the bass.
  • DRY 60, REV 40: low and far away. It should be felt more than heard.

Track 5

One note at the top of eight bars, then empty phrases, so the loop is struck once per pass of the progression and holds.

  1. Song view, track 5, row 00: chain 05. In it, phrase 50 on row 0 and an empty phrase 5F on rows 1 to 7.
  2. Phrase 50, row 0: C-4, V 50, instrument 0B.

Which note plays a sample at its own pitch. The manual's note that "notes above C-4 are limited" by bit depth suggests C-4 is where a sample plays as recorded, so these pages use it. Report back: does C-4 sound at the pitch you recorded?

Mute everything else and the texture should sound like a place, not a sound effect. Then bring the other tracks back: you should lose track of it within a bar, and miss it if you mute it. If you can hear the loop's seam, choose a calmer stretch, or try PLAY OSC on the card.

Things to try

  • DETUNE below 80, or play it on a lower note, for a darker, slower texture. See the Zoom M4 route on Sampling for pitching down a long way.
  • Give instrument 0B its own LFO on CUTOFF with FREQ 80, like the pad's, so the texture opens and closes with the tide.
