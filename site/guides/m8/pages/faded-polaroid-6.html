Faded Polaroid · step 6 of 10

The lead

The song's hook: a breathy flute-like line, high above everything, that keeps reaching up and settling one step short of home. It's the first melody in this guide, so this step is about writing one: what the notes do against the chords, and how a few FX commands shape the phrasing. The voice is the Polaroid lead card.

Listen forTwo halves with the same rhythm: a beat of silence, two eighths reaching up, a long note that slides in. Each half comes to rest on the "and" of 2 of its second bar, a small sigh, and neither lands on a root. In bar 8 the line climbs instead of settling.

Why it works

  • It rests on ninths. B over A in bar 2, A over G in bar 4. Your chords put a ninth on top of every bar; the hook joins them. It never lands on a root, so it never quite comes home. The one time it does is the song's last note, in step 10.
  • Every note is a chord tone of its bar, so it clashes with nothing. The one rub to listen for is bar 3, where the lead's D6 sits a minor ninth above the chord's top C♯5. If it bites, the cure is in the chord, not the hook.
  • Long notes against the arp's eighths. The arp is busy and low; the lead is slow and high, A5 to E6, an octave above the arp's top. The two never fight for the same space.

Three commands that shape the phrasing

  • No note on row 0 of 41 and 43. The long note from the bar before keeps sounding across the bar line. That's a tie.
  • OFF on row 0 of 40 and 42 ends the note before, so each half starts after a beat of silence. On the empty note (---), OPTION+EDIT inserts one. With the ADSR below, an OFF starts the release.
  • PSL 04 on the long notes: a four-tick slide up into E6 and D6 from the note before. A note with an instrument number resets PSL (changelog, 6.6.3), so it slides into that note only.

Instrument 04: the flute model

A Macrosynth on the BLOWN / FLUTED model: a reed or flute physical model where TIMBRE is the air pressure and COLOR the geometry of the instrument (manual). It has no filter of its own, so the M8's filter is the cassette's soft top.

  • TIMBRE 60: enough pressure to speak quickly on the eighth-note pickups, short of overblowing. COLOR 40: a short, wooden bore rather than a long metal one; set it by ear.
  • DEGRADE 08: a little worn-cassette grain. CUTOFF A8: breathy, not shrill, up here.
  • DRY B0, REV 70: soft enough to live above everything, and the song's biggest reverb send, so held notes leave a trace. DEL 30: dotted-eighth echoes between the long notes.
  • TBL. TIC 03 is for the table in step 7; set it now. EQ 04 is a low cut, set up in step 10; leave EQ at -- until then if you like.

The model's name and number on 6.6.3. 1F FLUTED is Braids' model order in hex, which matches every number your photos and the cards show (20 STRUCK BELL, 28 WAV PARAPHONIC). If it reads otherwise, 1E BLOWN, the reed model beside it, is the fallback. Report back: what does SHAPE read at 1E and 1F?

Whether the flute speaks on an eighth note. The manual doesn't say how quickly this model starts. Three mods

  • MOD 1, ADSR on VOLUME. ADSR times are seconds. Raise ATK until the help line reads about 0.030 s (about one tick), REL until it reads about 0.250 s, a short release on OFF. DEC 00 and SUS FF: it holds for as long as the note lasts. Since 6.6 a retrigger resumes from the current level, so one note joins the next: one breath a phrase.
  • MOD 2, AHD on TIMBRE: AMT 18, ATK 00, HOLD 00, DEC 06. A puff of extra air pressure for one sixteenth: the breath on the attack.
  • MOD 3, LFO on PITCH: SIN, FREE, AMT 01, FREQ 80. The arp's tape wobble, same depth and length: one tape for both.

The hex values for these ADSR times: the hex-to-seconds curve isn't published, and you've read three points off the screen (60 = 0.200 s, C2 = 2.869 s, CB = 3.663 s). Set them by the help line. Report back: which hex values give 0.030 and 0.250 s?

The demo plays the notes, the slides and the OFFs on a plain tone. The breath, the flute and the wobble are your M8's.

Things to try

  • TIMBRE 80: more pressure, more air, closer to overblowing.
  • PSL 08 into the long notes: lazier.
  • Take the OFF out of phrase 42: the two halves join into one long breath.
