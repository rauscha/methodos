Field recording loop

A sound from where you are (rain, a café, a train) recorded on the M8's own mic, looped low under everything. It's Low Tide's texture track (step 8), and it needs no computer, so it works on the trip. More on recording and samples: Sampling.

Record it

  1. On a free instrument, set TYPE to SAMPLER. Press EDIT on REC. to open the sample editor.
  2. Set SRC to MIC and bring VOL up until the loudest part sits high without clipping.
  3. Start with the power button rather than EDIT on START, so the key click isn't in the recording. Record half a minute or more.
  4. SELECT a calm stretch of 5 to 10 seconds with no one sound sticking out. Then PROCESS: CROP, then XFADE LOOP so the end blends into the start, then NORMALIZE.
  5. NAME it and SAVE. OPTION leaves the editor.

The instrument

  • PLAY FWDLOOP: loops forward for as long as the note holds.
  • FILTER HIGHPASS, CUTOFF 30: takes out the rumble (traffic, wind, handling) that would fight the bass.
  • DRY 60, REV 40: low and far away. It should be felt more than heard; if you notice it, it's too loud.

Play it

One note on row 0 of phrase 50, then seven empty phrases in the chain, so it's struck once every eight bars and holds.

Try

  • PLAY OSC: oscillator modes ignore the start position and don't restart on a new note, so the loop runs on through the chain without a seam at bar 1.
  • DETUNE or a lower note for a darker, slower texture. On the Zoom M4 at 96 kHz you can pitch down much further without grit.
  • A slow LFO on CUTOFF so the texture opens and closes with the pad.

In Overpass: the rain

Overpass (step 10) reuses this recording as rain under the whole song, as instrument 07, set duller and quieter, with an envelope so its OFF fades instead of cutting:

  • FILTER LOWPASS, CUTOFF 70 instead of HIGHPASS 30: wet, not crisp. The rumble the high-pass took out is now cut by EQ 07: a LOWCUT on the LOW band at about 200 Hz, STEREO.
  • TRANSP. OFF, so nothing moves it; DRY 58, REV 50, below the hats.
  • ADSR on VOLUME: ATK about 0.5 s, REL about 1.0 s, SUS FF, set by the help line. It fades in when struck and fades out over a second at an OFF.
