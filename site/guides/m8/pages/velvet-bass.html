Velvet bass

A warm, filtered saw with a little drive, fat enough to carry the roots under rootless chords. From the Faded Polaroid session, under Polaroid chords.

The browser plays the notes on a plain tone. Mute T1 and T3 to hear the bass alone.

Play it

The chord's root on row 0 of each bar. The chords leave the root out; this puts it back.

Why these values

  • SHAPE SAW, CUTOFF 60, RES 08: a saw with its top shaved off. Full harmonics low down, nothing fizzy.
  • AMP 18, LIM POST: a little gain, clipped after the filter, so it thickens without getting brighter.
  • AHD on volume: ATK 01, HOLD 18, DEC 30. At 90 BPM: holds one beat, then fades over two. The bass breathes instead of droning.
  • REV 08: almost none. Reverb on bass muddies the chord above it.

Try

  • SHAPE TRIANGLE if the saw is too aggressive.
  • A second note on row 0A, an octave up and quieter, for movement.
