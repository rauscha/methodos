Acid bass

A squelchy, resonant saw line in the 303 manner: every note opens the filter and snaps it shut, loud notes open it further, and a couple of notes slide into the next. A card about modulation: three mod slots doing three jobs.

The browser plays the notes on a plain tone, without the squelch or the slides.

Play it

One bar at 120 BPM in A minor. Loud notes (70) are the accents; PSL 06 on rows 6 and B glides into those notes.

Why these values

  • CUTOFF 30, RES C0: a nearly closed filter, ringing hard. On its own it's a dull hum; the envelope is what opens it.
  • MOD 1, AHD on CUTOFF: AMT 70, DEC 0C. Each note opens the filter and closes it over 12 ticks, an eighth note at any tempo. That's the "wow".
  • MOD 2, AHD on VOLUME: HOLD 04, DEC 08. Short notes with gaps between them, so the line bounces instead of droning.
  • MOD 3, TRACKING: SRC VELOCITY, DEST CUTOFF, LVAL 00, HVAL 40. Velocity also opens the filter, so the 70s scream and the 40s stay low.
  • PSL 06: portamento, in ticks. Six ticks is one row, so the slide takes the whole step.
  • AMP 20, LIM CLIP: a bit of grit.

Try

  • An LFO on CUTOFF, very slow (FREQ 40 or more), so the line opens over several bars.
  • SHAPE PULSE 50% for the square-wave flavor.
  • RES higher until it nearly whistles, then back off a hair.

Values are a starting point, not tried on an M8. Is the third mod type named TRACKING on your screen, and does a mod slot accept CUTOFF as DEST alongside another slot on CUTOFF?
