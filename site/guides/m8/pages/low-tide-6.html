The duck

The pad dips each time the kick hits, then swells back: the breathing you hear under most trip-hop. On a computer this is sidechain compression. On the M8 it's a trig envelope: an envelope on the pad that is fired by another instrument's notes. It's the meetups' own way of doing it; Tim demonstrated it as "a fake compressor" in the firmware 4.0 meetup.

The demo doesn't play the kick, but the kick's notes still fire the duck, so you hear the pad dip where the kick would be.

MOD 3 on the pad

Instrument 03 already uses MOD 1 (the ADSR) and MOD 2 (the LFO). SHIFT+UP from the instrument, and set up the third slot:

SettingWhy
MOD3 TRIG ENVAn AHD envelope (attack, hold, decay) that another instrument fires.
DEST VOLUMEIt moves the pad's level.
SRC 06Fired by instrument 06, the kick. SRC 00–7F is an instrument; 80–87 is every instrument on tracks 1 to 8. Track 1 also has the snare, so SRC 80 would duck on the snares too. Naming the instrument ducks on kicks only.
AMT, negativeA trig envelope is bipolar: a negative amount pulls the level down. Aim for about half.
ATK 00The dip is instant.
HOLD 03Stays down for 3 ticks, half a row.
DEC 12Comes back over 18 ticks (12 in hex), three rows: most of a beat.

Its times are ticks, like any AHD, not seconds like the pad's ADSR. So the duck stays locked to the beat at any tempo.

How a negative AMT is written. The manual says only that a trig envelope "can have a positive or negative amount applied". Raise AMT from 00 and watch the help line. Report back: the AMT value, as the screen shows it, whose help line reads about minus half.

Listen forThe pad should sink on beat 1 and on the "and" of 3, and float back up in between. Too deep and it pumps like dance music; aim for a breath you'd miss if it stopped. In the M8's breakdown (step 9), the kick drops out and the pad stops breathing, which is part of why the breakdown feels open.

Things to try

  • DEC 18 (24 ticks, a full beat): a slower, more obvious swell back.
  • SRC 80: ducks on every note on track 1, kick and snare. Busier.
  • The same trig envelope on the bass (instrument 02, MOD 3 is free), with a smaller AMT, so bass and kick don't stack.
