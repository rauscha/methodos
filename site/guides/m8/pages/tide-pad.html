Tide pad

A warm, wide pad that takes a bar to arrive, fades over two, and slowly dulls and opens on an eight-bar cycle. It plays one held note and changes chords with CRD, so nothing re-attacks. From Low Tide, step 2, with the chord banks from step 3. Mute T2 to hear it alone.

What shapes it

Notes

  • Needs firmware 6.6 for SOFT SAW.
  • SWARM 08, not more. SWARM is a wide detune (about a fifth up and down at 80), and past about 10 the pad starts to buzz, worse on close chords.
  • ATK and REL are set by the help line, in seconds: 2.86 s and 5.71 s are one bar and two bars at 84 BPM. At another tempo, set them again. The LFO counts steps, so the tide stays eight bars at any tempo.
  • SUBOSC 80 and SHIFT 80 are the defaults: no sub, both halves of the chord at full level.
  • The ADSR only releases on an OFF. End a section with an OFF a couple of bars before it should be silent.
  • Plays rootless: put the root on another track. The banks are intervals above a held D-3; over a D, bank 00 is Dm9.
  • In step 6 it gets a third mod, a trig envelope that ducks it on each kick.
