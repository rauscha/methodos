Tide pad

A warm, wide pad that takes a bar to arrive, fades over two, and slowly dulls and opens on an eight-bar cycle. Built for slow music where the chords change every bar or two. From Low Tide, step 2.

What shapes it

Notes

  • Needs firmware 6.6 for SOFT SAW and for the smooth retrigger on chord changes.
  • The ADSR only releases on an OFF. End a section with an OFF a couple of bars before it should be silent.
  • Timing is in ticks, so the swell and the tide stay one bar and eight bars at any tempo.
  • Plays rootless: put the root on another track. Bank 00 (F A C E on F-3) over a D is Dm9.
