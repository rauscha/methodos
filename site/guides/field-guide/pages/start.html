A guide is a folder

Every guide in Methodos is a folder of HTML pages plus one small file, guide.json, that puts them in order. The app itself knows nothing about any subject. Adding a guide never changes the app.

What's in the folder

site/guides/m8/
  guide.json        title, sections, and the pages in each
  pages/
    tables-101.html   one artifact, exactly as Claude or ChatGPT made it
    acid-bass.html

Sections

A guide is split into sections. Each section has a layout:

  • steps: a numbered sequence you work through in order. Each step shows its number in an open ring, or a check in a filled disc once you mark it done.
  • cards: a grid you browse in any order, like a shelf of patch cards.

Sections can share a group heading. A song that takes five artifacts can be its own steps section, grouped with the other songs under one heading.

Tap a section's heading to fold it shut, and tap again to open it. A folded section's marker points right and its heading says show; an open one points down and says hide. Each device remembers which sections you folded, so an introduction you've read stays out of the way.

On the library, Arrange (at the bottom) puts Up, Down and Hide under each guide. A hidden guide moves to the folded Hidden guides list at the foot of the library, where Show brings it back. The order is this device's own.

Working through a page

Open a page and use the bar at the bottom to move to the previous or next page in the same section (the arrow keys work too). Mark done keeps your place, and the guide page offers Continue back to wherever you left off.
