## 1. The song (harmony, M8 accuracy)

Verified correct: all five bank spellings and chord names (bank 03 = A7sus4♭9, bank 04 = A7♭9
as a rootless E°7); the bar 8→1 "every voice up" claim (E→F, G→A, B♭→C, C♯→D); the top-voice
line (D, E, D×5, C♯); "A#1"; SHIFT+arrow navigation and Project above Song; SHIFT+UP to mods;
empty phrase vs empty chain row; ADSR releases only on OFF; 24 ticks a quarter, 96 a bar; 0x60 =
96 and 0xC0 = 192; DRY/MFX/DEL/REV; clone via SHIFT+OPTION then EDIT; EDIT+UP for the FX help
list; "set = add" holds because every chord row has I=03, which resets parameters.

1. **High. The octave convention is unverified and every note depends on it.** The kit treats
   C-4 as MIDI 60. The manual calls C-1 "the lowest octave" and says the N column spans "11
   octaves, the entire 128 MIDI note range": read literally, C-4 would be MIDI 36, two octaves
   below the preview; tracker convention (C-0 = MIDI 0) would be one below. Either way the pad
   lands low and G-1 is sub-audible. **Fix:** 30-second device check: enter C-4 on a plain
   Macrosynth and compare to a 261 Hz reference (phone tuner); then set `midiOf` to the M8's
   mapping and re-spell the spec.
2. **High. LFO FREQ is a rate, not a period.** The manual: "the rate at which the LFO cycles,
   represented in steps". Higher is probably faster; the kit models it as a period, so the page
   and the preview agree with each other and likely not with the device. **Fix:** fallback text
   "try 02–04"; report-back asks "does raising FREQ speed it up or slow it down?"
3. **Med.** "EDIT twice makes a new phrase" is right but inserts the lowest *unused* number, not
   31–34. Fix: "EDIT, then dial 31 with EDIT+UP/RIGHT" or "accept whatever number appears".
4. **Med. Bank 02 (F A B♭ D) puts A3 and B♭3 a semitone apart** inside detuned saw pairs; with
   SWARM 30 it will beat and sound sour. Fix: offer the three-voice `00 05 09` (F B♭ D, top D
   kept) as the first "try" on page 3.
5. **Med. A cheaper mechanism is missed:** an empty I column changes notes without
   retriggering (manual line 639). If the bank command is absolute, phrases 31–34 can drop I and
   the envelope question disappears. Caveat: with no I, relative commands aren't reset, so the
   "set = add" argument then fails. Fix: test this alongside the command's name.
6. **Med. Musical judgment.** i–♭VI–iv–V7♭9 at 84 is noir/cinematic more than trip-hop; a full
   bar of A7♭9 is the loop's most generic moment, the sus4♭9 bar its most interesting. **Cheap
   fix:** move the C♯ late in bar 8 (phrase 34 note on row 0C, bank 03 until then) so it's a push
   into bar 1, not a bar-long dominant. Register: pad at F3–E4 is tenor; try F-4 shapes as the
   one-octave "try".
7. **Low.** "Hold PLAY in the instrument view and change CUTOFF": PLAY starts the phrase;
   EDIT+PLAY previews the instrument. Fix: "press PLAY, then EDIT+LEFT/RIGHT on CUTOFF".
8. **Low.** "The 6.6 smooth retrigger only applies within one instrument" is an assumption. Mark
   unverified.
9. **Low.** "AMT 40 for about an octave either side" is the kit's demo scale, not an M8 fact.
10. **Low.** SUBOSC 00 = no sub is supported by the manual; drop it from the unverified box.
11. **Low.** PAN isn't in the manual's per-instrument lists (AMP, LIM, DRY, MFX, DEL, REV); his
    photo shows PAN 80 on 6.6.3, so keep but mark.
12. **Low.** Page 1 "two bars per chord": bars 1–2 are two voicings. Say "two bars per root".

## 2. The design (panels, pages, protanopia, phone)

No hue-only state found: current row, new, now/next, muted and sounding all survive grayscale.
The failures are contrast and layout.

1. **High. Light `--m8-mid` (#7d776b) fails small-text contrast**: 4.3:1 on paper, 3.8:1 on
   card, and it carries every secondary label (canvas axes at 10–11 px, phrase headers, row
   numbers, legends, Roman numerals, kicker). `.m8-dim` at 0.75 opacity drops to ~3:1. Fix:
   light mid → ~#6b665a (the app shell's `--muted` #6e685c is already there); drop the opacity
   on `.m8-dim`. Dark mid (#948d7f, 5.2:1) is fine.
2. **High. Five 16-row phrase tables in a row** on low-tide-3 (~2,800 px, 3.5 screens of
   "--- 00") between the instruction and the chain. Fix: a `compact` mode, default when only row
   0 is filled (one data row + "rows 1–F empty"); better, one combined screen with a row per
   phrase. Full 16 rows only where a phrase is first introduced (low-tide-2).
3. **High. Chord-lane labels still collide** in the dark roll ("A7sus4A7♭9" overprinted at bars
   7–8); the stagger depends on measured width. Fix: clip each label to its bar span and, when it
   doesn't fit, show only the bold current chord. The lane duplicates the cards anyway.
4. **High. Filter spectrum bars drawn in `--m8-faint`** (~1.5:1 light, ~1.9:1 dark), the only
   picture of "what this track is playing". Fix: fill with mid; the ink 2.5 px curve still
   separates by weight.
5. **Medium. Sticky transport wraps to two rows** on low-tide-3 (~95 px, 12% of the viewport,
   permanently); buttons 36 px. Fix: one row, mutes labeled `T2`/`T3` (name in aria-label),
   min-height 44 px, fixed-width mute labels (don't append "(muted)"; dashed + strikethrough
   already carry it). Consider sticking it to the bottom for thumb reach.
6. **Medium. Roll top-voice line** sits 3 px above the block in the same ink as the outlines, so
   it reads as a thicker edge. Light-mode hatch (faint on paper) is invisible though the legend
   promises it. Fix: draw the line through the block centre with a paper halo (paper 5 px, then
   ink 3 px); hatch with mid.
7. **Medium. Envelope "SUS" label sits under the live dot**; no time ticks on envelope or
   filter-over-time though the copy is in bars. Fix: labels below the baseline; tick each bar.
8. **Medium. low-tide-3 rhythm:** cards (~900 px) + roll (~840 px) before the first h2, same six
   chords twice; first "Listen for" 10+ screens after Play. Fix: cards as a compact one-line list
   (NOW/NEXT as a prefix word); a short Listen box right under the roll.
9. **Medium. "···" for the unknown command** looks like the "---" empty placeholder, and stacks a
   dotted underline on a solid one. Fix: bold `???`; drop the solid underline on `.m8-is-new`
   (NEW tag + margin bar already mark it).
10. **Low.** NEW tag repeated 7× in chain 03: tag the first of a run, or "rows 1–7 new" in the
    caption.
11. **Low.** Instrument grid lists ten untaught defaults, then the why-table repeats the taught
    ones. Show only params the step sets; "others at default".
12. **Low.** Canvas text at 10–11 px: minimum 12, 13 for the chord lane.

## 4. The plan (what to cut, reorder, or doubt)

1. **High. The premise is untested.** Everything (engine voices, spec format, six panels, prompt
   pack) rests on "live panels plus a browser stand-in teach more than text plus a tracker
   mirror", and he hasn't pressed Play once. **Freeze building.** The one question to get back by
   text: *"Did any picture tell you something the words didn't? Which one?"* If the answer is
   "the envelope" or "none", the roadmap changes shape.
2. **High. Step 3 hangs on an unverified mechanism:** Hypersynth bank switching by command, and
   the "base CHORD 00" trick. Song 2 inherits it. **Ask for a 10-minute check first:** one
   phrase, two banks, the I column vs. an FX command, a photo of the FX list on a Hypersynth
   phrase. Make it the first thing `low-tide-3` asks.
3. **High. Scope.** Defer until after slice-1 feedback: Tone.js vendoring (the kit already works
   without it; the plan is stale), the braids.wasm Macrosynth port, the CC0 sample library, drum
   grid, loop ring, Tables tutorial, Songs 2–3, patch cards beyond the pad. `AUTHORING.md` is
   building for the builder; he never reads it. Keep only what steps 4–5 need, and only after
   his reaction.
4. **Medium. Sound.** Envelope and filter earn sound: shape is audible. Imitating the Hypersynth
   tone doesn't: a thin browser pad invites "this song is meh" and he judges the harmony by the
   stand-in. **Option (c):** sound for step 2's swell and tide; steps 1 and 3 play the chords on
   a plain triangle labeled "the notes, not the tone". No new voices until he says sound helped.
5. **Medium. Sequencing.** Pad-before-chords is right for building a patch, but step 1 must
   already play the eight bars on a plain tone so the destination exists first. (It does: the
   step-1 player is set to step 3.)
6. **Medium. Panels.** Roll with the top-voice line teaches the pedal tone; the progression strip
   duplicates it for five chords, drop one. Envelope with gate and filter-over-time teach. Loop
   ring is decoration; a 16-row phrase already reads as a loop. **Missing** for how he thinks: a
   staff view (deferred) and a **units readout** (ticks and steps to beats and seconds at the
   song tempo), since FREQ-in-steps and ADSR-in-ticks are the confusing part.
7. **Medium. Unverified boxes state doubt but request nothing.** End each with one answerable
   ask: a yes/no, a value as the screen shows it, or "photo of screen X". One "Report back" list,
   five items max, on the intro page.
8. **Low. Offline.** Test "Save for offline" on the Pixel in airplane mode before any further
   work. (The scripted test passed; the phone hasn't been tried.)

**Cheapest trip test for 1 and 2 together:** he works step 2 only, builds the pad on the
device, and replies with three sentences and one photo.
