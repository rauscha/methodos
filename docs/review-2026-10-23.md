## 4. The plan (what to cut, reorder, or doubt)

1. **High. The premise is untested.** Everything (engine voices, spec format, six panels, prompt
   pack) rests on "live panels plus a browser stand-in teach more than text plus a tracker
   mirror", and he hasn't pressed Play once. **Freeze building.** The one question to get back by
   text: *"Did any picture tell you something the words didn't? Which one?"* If the answer is
   "the envelope" or "none", the roadmap changes shape.
2. **High. Step 3 hangs on an unverified mechanism:** Hypersynth bank switching by command, and
   the "base CHORD 00" trick. Song 2 inherits it. **Ask for a 10-minute check first:** one
   phrase, two banks, the I column vs. an FX command, a photo of the FX list on a Hypersynth
   phrase. Make it the first thing `low-tide-3` asks.
3. **High. Scope.** Defer until after slice-1 feedback: Tone.js vendoring (the kit already works
   without it; the plan is stale), the braids.wasm Macrosynth port, the CC0 sample library, drum
   grid, loop ring, Tables tutorial, Songs 2–3, patch cards beyond the pad. `AUTHORING.md` is
   building for the builder; he never reads it. Keep only what steps 4–5 need, and only after
   his reaction.
4. **Medium. Sound.** Envelope and filter earn sound: shape is audible. Imitating the Hypersynth
   tone doesn't: a thin browser pad invites "this song is meh" and he judges the harmony by the
   stand-in. **Option (c):** sound for step 2's swell and tide; steps 1 and 3 play the chords on
   a plain triangle labeled "the notes, not the tone". No new voices until he says sound helped.
5. **Medium. Sequencing.** Pad-before-chords is right for building a patch, but step 1 must
   already play the eight bars on a plain tone so the destination exists first. (It does: the
   step-1 player is set to step 3.)
6. **Medium. Panels.** Roll with the top-voice line teaches the pedal tone; the progression strip
   duplicates it for five chords, drop one. Envelope with gate and filter-over-time teach. Loop
   ring is decoration; a 16-row phrase already reads as a loop. **Missing** for how he thinks: a
   staff view (deferred) and a **units readout** (ticks and steps to beats and seconds at the
   song tempo), since FREQ-in-steps and ADSR-in-ticks are the confusing part.
7. **Medium. Unverified boxes state doubt but request nothing.** End each with one answerable
   ask: a yes/no, a value as the screen shows it, or "photo of screen X". One "Report back" list,
   five items max, on the intro page.
8. **Low. Offline.** Test "Save for offline" on the Pixel in airplane mode before any further
   work. (The scripted test passed; the phone hasn't been tried.)

**Cheapest trip test for 1 and 2 together:** he works step 2 only, builds the pad on the
device, and replies with three sentences and one photo.
